Why we're different

A lake house is not a subdivision house

Waterfront and near-water homes carry a humidity load that inland rules of thumb simply miss. Oversize the equipment — which is the easy, lazy, profitable thing to do — and it short-cycles, never runs long enough to pull moisture out, and leaves you with a cold, clammy house and a musty crawlspace.

  • Manual J load calculations on every replacement — not a guess based on the box that's already there.
  • Variable-speed equipment that runs long and low, which is how you actually control humidity.
  • Corrosion-aware siting and coil coatings for units that sit in constant damp shade near the water.
  • Crawlspace and duct evaluation included, because that's where half of lake-house comfort problems actually live.
