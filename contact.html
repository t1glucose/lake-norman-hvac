Before you call

Two things worth trying first

My system stopped cooling entirely

Check the thermostat batteries, then check your breaker panel — both the indoor and outdoor breakers. Then look at the filter; a completely blocked filter can freeze the coil solid. If the coil is iced, switch the system to FAN ONLY for an hour to thaw it before we arrive. If none of that does it, call us.

Water is dripping near the indoor unit

That is almost always a blocked condensate drain. Shut the system off at the thermostat to stop it making more water, put a towel down, and call us. Left alone it will find your ceiling.
